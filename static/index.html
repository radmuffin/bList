About bList

v0.1.0 Active 🏆 Master Wanderer

Visual Map Bucket List & Trip Planner

A fast, private, and deterministic travel bucket list application. Easily save places from Google Maps, Instagram, Apple Maps, and travel articles, organize trips into custom collections, and explore on an interactive map.

Core Tenets of bList

01
Pin First, Wander Forever

Save places whenever inspiration strikes. Real adventures start with simple pins.

02
Fast, Reliable & Deterministic

Precise OpenStreetMap coordinates and instant previews. Real spots that accurately exist without fluff or delay.

03
Back-Alley Discoveries Rule

The best hidden gems and noodle shops are found by curious travelers, not sponsored ad feeds.

04
Your Data, Your Journey

Private multi-device sync, zero trackers, full offline PWA support, and open GeoJSON/CSV exports.

Trip Planning Toolkit

⚡
1-Click Turn-by-Turn Routes

Instant TSP 2-Opt route optimizer and multi-stop Google Maps directions for road trips and day walking tours.

📅
Day-by-Day Itineraries

Organize places into Day 1, Day 2, Day 3 groups with must-see ⭐ priority flags and custom trip icons.

🕒
Live Opening Hours

Real-time indicators showing whether a venue is Open, Closing Soon, or Open 24/7 before you head out.

👥
Real-Time Collaboration

Share private collection invite links and see avatar badges of fellow travelers planning together.

Engine & Performance

Backend Engine Rust & Axum 0.7 ⚡
Database SQLite (WAL Mode)
Map Engine OpenStreetMap & Leaflet
Frontend Vanilla ES6+ • Zero Build
